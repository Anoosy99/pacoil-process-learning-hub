STEP 01

Incoming oil

The oil enters the bleaching section from the previous stage of the refinery.

WHAT TO NOTICE
  • Incoming flow rate
  • Feed temperature
  • Source tank and pump status
Key idea

The pump provides the force that moves oil through the pipe.

The whole process in one sentence

Crude oil is heated and mixed with bleaching earth in the bleaching section, sent through one of four pressure filters, then continues through the VE702/E702 heat-and-vacuum section before storage or the next process.

Training rule

An alarm is information requiring attention—not permission to operate equipment. Observe, report, and follow the authorised procedure. Never press ACK, START, STOP, RESET, AUTO or MANUAL without approval.
